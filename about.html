Our Story

Café Mosaic was founded in 2018 with a simple dream — to create a warm space where people can connect, relax, and enjoy quality coffee. Every detail, from our locally sourced beans to our handmade pastries, reflects our love for craft and community.

Whether you're here for your morning espresso or a cozy afternoon chat, Café Mosaic is your home away from home.
